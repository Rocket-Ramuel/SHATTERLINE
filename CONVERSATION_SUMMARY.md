## 1. The original request

> "@roguelite_prompt.md Remember, DON'T THINK ABOUT BEING ECONOMICAL WITH TOKENS WHILE WORKING BUT MAKE THE CODE ECONOMICAL"

**My reaction:** I built the whole game described in `roguelite_prompt.md`: a fast top-down twin-stick roguelite in a single `index.html` file that needs nothing else to run.

**What I built:**
- **Core game:** smooth movement, dashing, aiming and shooting, with game "feel" effects like screen shake, brief freeze-frames on big hits, and slow motion on big moments.
- **Content:** 13 weapons, 5 characters, about 90 upgrades, 24 synergies (bonus effects when you combine certain upgrades) and 8 active items.
- **Enemies:** 17 enemy types with real tactics (flanking, taking cover, working as a pack), plus a "director" that controls how hard each wave pushes you.
- **World:** 4 sectors, each with a boss, and a branching map with shops, treasure rooms, rest stops and random events.
- **Sound:** all music and sound effects are generated by code, with music that reacts to the action.
- **Visuals:** glow, colour-fringe and old-CRT-screen effects.
- **Meta-progression:** unlocks, achievements, a codex, run history, daily seeds and difficulty levels after your first win.
- **Performance:** built to stay smooth with 300+ enemies and 1000+ bullets on screen.
- **Testing and docs:** I tested it with automated bots and screenshots, and wrote two documents for future work, `DESIGN.md` and `PROGRESS.md`.

| **"I beat the game in 10 minutes… pyromancer + homing rockets… held fire"** | The cause was that every flamer tick could launch rockets and trigger other on-hit effects, so fast-firing weapons multiplied everything. Now each weapon's chance to trigger effects is weighted by how much damage one shot does, so firing faster no longer means more effects. Explosions also can't set off more explosions. Your build's damage against groups dropped from about 1070 to about 430. |
| **"Unstoppable in the third room"** | The difficulty now adapts to you. If you clear rooms quickly without getting hit, enemies get more health and bigger waves. If you're struggling, it eases off. | **"Add more ways to heal + base regen ~1 HP per 3 s"** | Base regeneration is exactly 1 HP every 3 seconds. Healing beyond max HP becomes a temporary shield. |
| **"More curses, feel unique, not upgrades with drawbacks"** | Curses are now **Pacts**, 13 of them. Each one changes the rules of the run in exchange for a big reward. For example, **Hunted** sends an unkillable Stalker after you, **Doomsday** puts a countdown on every room, **Frenzy** keeps your weapon firing nonstop and **Darkness** shrinks your light. Rest sites can remove a Pact. |
